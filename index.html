Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to see results on LinkedIn? ▼

Most clients see initial results within 4-6 weeks of implementing our strategy. However, consistent growth typically accelerates after 3-4 months as you build momentum and establish your thought leadership presence.

What's included in the free audit? ▼

The free audit includes a comprehensive analysis of your current LinkedIn profile, visibility audit, content strategy recommendations, and a personalized growth roadmap tailored to your goals.

Can you help with personal or business LinkedIn accounts? ▼

Yes, we work with both personal profiles and business/company accounts. Our strategies are customized based on your specific goals, whether you're building personal brand authority or growing your company's presence.

How often should I post on LinkedIn? ▼

We recommend a consistent posting schedule of 2-3 times per week for optimal results. Quality matters more than quantity, so we focus on creating valuable content that resonates with your audience rather than posting excessively.

What if I don't know what to post about? ▼

That's exactly what we help with! We develop content pillars based on your expertise and create a content calendar with specific post ideas, headlines, and engagement strategies tailored to your niche.
